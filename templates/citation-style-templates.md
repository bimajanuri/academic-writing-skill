# Citation & Bibliography Style Templates (APA 7, MLA 9, Turabian, Vancouver, Harvard, Chicago, IEEE)

Apply the user's selected style consistently across all in-text citations and `bibliography.md`. Verify every entry against `papers.json`.

---

## 1. APA 7th Edition (Author-Date — Psychology, Education, Social Sciences)

- **In-Text**:
  - *1 author*: `(Smith, 2025)` or `Smith (2025)` | *Direct quote*: `(Smith, 2025, p. 42)`
  - *2 authors*: `(Smith & Pratama, 2025)` (parenthetical) vs. `Smith and Pratama (2025)` (narrative)
  - *3+ authors*: `(Rahman et al., 2024)` from first citation onward
  - *Multiple sources*: `(Chen, 2023; Putnam & Lee, 2024; Santoso et al., 2025)` (alphabetical, semicolon-separated)
- **Reference List (Alphabetical, hanging indent, sentence-case article titles, Title-Case journal names, up to 20 authors before `...`)**:
  - *Journal Article*: `Surname, A. A., & Surname, B. B. (2025). Title of the article: Subtitle in sentence case. Journal Title in Italics, 14(2), 112–128. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`
  - *Book*: `Surname, A. A. (2024). Book title in sentence case (2nd ed.). Publisher Name. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`
  - *Edited Chapter*: `Surname, A. A. (2024). Chapter title. In E. E. Editor & F. F. Editor (Eds.), Book title in sentence case (pp. 45–68). Publisher. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`
  - *Conference Paper*: `Surname, A. A., & Surname, B. B. (2025). Paper title. In Proceedings of the Conference Name (pp. 10–19). Publisher. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`

## 2. IEEE (Numbered Square Brackets — Engineering, Computer Science, AI)

- **In-Text**: Numbered sequentially in order of first appearance: `[1]`, `[2], [3]`, `[4]–[7]`. With page number: `[1, p. 15]`. Never write "in [1]" as a noun only if avoidable; prefer `"Smith et al. [1] demonstrated..."` or `"...as shown in [1]."`.
- **Reference List (Numerical order `[1], [2]...`, initials before surname, quotation marks around article title in sentence case, abbreviated italicized journal title)**:
  - *Journal Article*: `[1] A. A. Surname, B. B. Surname, and C. C. Surname, "Title of article in sentence case," Abbrev. J. Title, vol. 14, no. 2, pp. 112–128, Feb. 2025, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
  - *Conference Paper*: `[2] A. A. Surname and B. B. Surname, "Paper title," in Proc. Int. Conf. Name (ACRONYM), City, Country, 2025, pp. 10–19, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
  - *Book*: `[3] A. A. Surname, Book Title in Title Case, 2nd ed. City, Country: Publisher, 2024.`

---

## 3. Vancouver (Numbered — Medicine, Biomedical & Health Sciences)

- **In-Text**: Sequential Arabic numerals in parentheses `(1)` or `(1–3)` or `(1,4)` in order of first appearance.
- **Reference List (Numerical order, surname + initials without periods/commas, list first 6 authors then `et al.`, NLM journal abbreviations without italics)**:
  - *Journal Article*: `1. Surname AA, Surname BB, Surname CC. Title of article in sentence case. Abbrev J Title. 2025;14(2):112–28. doi:10.xxxx/xxxxx`
  - *Book*: `2. Surname AA. Book title in sentence case. 2nd ed. City: Publisher; 2024.`

---

## 4. Chicago Manual of Style 17th Ed. (Author-Date & Notes-Bibliography)

- **Author-Date In-Text**: `(Smith 2025, 42)` or `(Rahman, Pratama, and Lee 2024)` (4+ authors: `(Santoso et al. 2025)`). No comma between author and year.
- **Author-Date Reference List (Alphabetical, full given names, Title Case for both article and journal titles)**:
  - *Journal Article*: `Surname, Firstname, and Firstname Surname. 2025. "Title of Article in Title Case." Journal Title in Title Case 14 (2): 112–28. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
  - *Book*: `Surname, Firstname. 2024. Book Title in Title Case. City: Publisher.`
- **Notes-Bibliography (Humanities / History)**:
  - *Footnote*: `1. Firstname Surname, "Title of Article," Journal Title 14, no. 2 (2025): 115, https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
  - *Bibliography*: `Surname, Firstname. "Title of Article." Journal Title 14, no. 2 (2025): 112–28. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.`

---

## 5. Turabian 9th Ed. (Theses, Dissertations, Theology, Humanities)

- Follows Chicago 17th in two variants:
  - **Notes-Bibliography**: Superscript note numbers in text `...claim.¹`; full note at first citation (`Firstname Surname, Book Title (City: Publisher, 2024), 45.`), shortened note thereafter (`Surname, Short Title, 45.`), and full Alphabetical Bibliography at end.
  - **Author-Date**: `(Surname 2025, 45)` in text + Alphabetical Reference List identical to Chicago Author-Date.

---

## 6. MLA 9th Edition (Author-Page — Literature, Cultural Studies, Media)

- **In-Text**: `(Surname 42)` or `(Surname and Surname 115)` or `(Surname et al. 18)`. If no page numbers: `(Surname)`.
- **Works Cited (Alphabetical, full given names, Title Case for articles and journals)**:
  - *Journal Article*: `Surname, Firstname, and Firstname Surname. "Title of Article in Title Case." Journal Title, vol. 14, no. 2, 2025, pp. 112–28, https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
  - *Book*: `Surname, Firstname. Book Title in Title Case. Publisher, 2024.`

---

## 7. Harvard (Author-Date — Business, Management, UK/Commonwealth Social Sciences)

- **In-Text**: `(Surname, 2025, p. 42)` or `(Surname and Surname, 2025)` or `(Surname et al., 2024)` (for 3+ authors).
- **Reference List (Alphabetical, surname + initials, single quotes around sentence-case article title, italicized journal title)**:
  - *Journal Article*: `Surname, A.A. and Surname, B.B. (2025) 'Title of article in sentence case', Journal Title in Italics, 14(2), pp. 112–128. doi:10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
  - *Book*: `Surname, A.A. (2024) Book title in italics. 2nd edn. City: Publisher.`
