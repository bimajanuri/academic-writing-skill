# Mode Auto-Validation Reporting Checklists & Stage Quality Checklists

Save the completed mode reporting checklist to `reporting_checklist_result.md`. Every item marked `No` or `Partial` requires a concrete remediation plan before Stage 7 completes.

## 1. PRISMA 2020 Checklist (SLR Mode — 27 Items, Page et al., 2021)
- **Title (1)**: `#1` Identify the report as a systematic review.
- **Abstract (2)**: `#2` Structured summary (background, objectives, sources, eligibility, appraisal, synthesis, results, limitations, conclusions, registration).
- **Introduction (3–4)**: `#3` Rationale in context of existing knowledge; `#4` Explicit objectives/questions framed via PICO/PICOS/PICo/SPIDER.
- **Methods (5–15)**: `#5` Eligibility criteria + rationale; `#6` Information sources + search dates; `#7` Full search strategies; `#8` Selection/screening process; `#9` Data collection/extraction process; `#10a` Outcomes sought; `#10b` Other variables extracted; `#11` Study risk of bias tool; `#12` Effect measures; `#13a–f` Synthesis methods (eligibility, preparation, display, model, heterogeneity, sensitivity); `#14` Reporting bias assessment; `#15` Certainty assessment (GRADE/CERQual).
- **Results (16–22)**: `#16a` Study selection flow counts + PRISMA diagram; `#16b` Excluded borderline studies + reasons; `#17` Study characteristics table; `#18` Risk of bias per study; `#19` Individual study results; `#20a–d` Synthesis results (characteristics, direction, heterogeneity, robustness); `#21` Reporting biases; `#22` Certainty of evidence.
- **Discussion (23)**: `#23a` Interpretation in context of other evidence; `#23b` Evidence limitations; `#23c` Review process limitations; `#23d` Practice, policy, and future research implications.
- **Other (24–27)**: `#24a–c` Registration/protocol; `#25` Funding; `#26` Competing interests; `#27` Data/`papers.json` availability.

## 2. PRISMA-ScR Checklist (Scoping Review Mode — 22 Items, Tricco et al., 2018)
- **Title & Abstract (1–2)**: `#1` Identify as a scoping review; `#2` Structured summary.
- **Introduction (3–4)**: `#3` Rationale; `#4` Objectives and questions framed via **PCC** (Population, Concept, Context).
- **Methods (5–13)**: `#5` Protocol/registration; `#6` Eligibility criteria + rationale; `#7` Information sources + search date; `#8` Full electronic search strategy for >= 1 database; `#9` Source selection process; `#10` Data charting process; `#11` Data items charted; `#12` *(Optional)* Critical appraisal of sources (state tool or state not performed); `#13` Synthesis/mapping methods.
- **Results (14–18)**: `#14` Selection flow counts + exclusion reasons; `#15` Characteristics of sources; `#16` *(Optional)* Critical appraisal results; `#17` Findings of individual sources; `#18` Synthesis of results (Conceptual Mapping taxonomy, distribution tables, gap matrix).
- **Discussion & Funding (19–22)**: `#19` Summary of evidence; `#20` Limitations; `#21` Conclusions & implications; `#22` Funding.

## 3. eMERGe Checklist (Meta-Ethnography Mode — 19 Criteria across 7 Phases, France et al., 2019)
- **Phase 1 (1–4)**: `#1` Rationale for meta-ethnography; `#2` Context and aims; `#3` Review question(s) (PICo/SPIDER); `#4` Protocol modifications.
- **Phase 2 (5–8)**: `#5` Search strategy rationale; `#6` Search processes; `#7` Selection criteria (interpretive qualitative studies); `#8` Screening outcome & flow.
- **Phase 3 (9–10)**: `#9` Reading and quality appraisal approach; `#10` Characteristics and context of included studies.
- **Phase 4 (11–12)**: `#11` Extraction and comparison of **1st-order** (participant quotes) and **2nd-order** (author themes) constructs; `#12` Relationship across studies (`Reciprocal`, `Refutational`, or `Line-of-Argument`).
- **Phase 5 (13–14)**: `#13` Step-by-step translation process; `#14` Translation outcome tables.
- **Phase 6 (15–16)**: `#15` Synthesis process for **3rd-order constructs** and line-of-argument model; `#16` Reviewer reflexivity.
- **Phase 7 (17–19)**: `#17` Summary of interpretive findings + traceable `1st -> 2nd -> 3rd order` chain; `#18` Strengths, limitations, reflexivity impact; `#19` Recommendations and conclusions.

## 4. SANRA Checklist (Narrative Review Mode — 6 Scored Items, Baethge et al., 2019)
Score each item `0` (absent), `1` (partial), or `2` (complete) — **Pass threshold: >= 10 / 12, no `0` items**:
1. Justification of the article's importance for the readership.
2. Statement of concrete aims or formulation of questions.
3. Description of the literature search (databases, years, keywords, inclusion logic).
4. Referencing (all competing claims backed by primary citations).
5. Scientific reasoning (Academic Debate synthesis weighing study designs and competing camps).
6. Appropriate presentation of data (comparative tables, timelines, or study outcomes).

## 5. Whittemore & Knafl Checklist (Integrative Review Mode — 17 Criteria, 2005)
- **Stage 1 — Problem Identification (1–3)**: `#1` Problem & purpose; `#2` Target variables/concepts; `#3` Rationale for combining qual + quant streams.
- **Stage 2 — Literature Search (4–6)**: `#4` Search strategy across >= 3 databases; `#5` Diverse-design eligibility criteria; `#6` Search flow documented.
- **Stage 3 — Data Evaluation (7–9)**: `#7` Design-specific rigor appraisal tool (MMAT or 2-point scale); `#8` Scores in matrix; `#9` Analytic use of quality scores.
- **Stage 4 — Data Analysis (10–14)**: `#10` *Data Reduction* (subgroups); `#11` *Data Display* (unified matrix); `#12` *Data Comparison* (cross-stream pattern comparison); `#13` *Conclusion Drawing* (integrative abstraction); `#14` *Verification* (primary-source check & discrepancy resolution).
- **Stage 5 — Presentation (15–17)**: `#15` Integrative model/table; `#16` Limitations of combining streams; `#17` Theory, practice, and research implications.

## 6. Critical Review Checklist (Critical Mode — 10 Criteria)
`#1` Explicit critical aim & scope; `#2` Transparent selection of seminal/representative studies; `#3` Epistemological & theoretical assumptions exposed; `#4` Methodological & sampling flaws appraised; `#5` Measurement validity critiques documented; `#6` Systemic biases analyzed; `#7` Synthesized by critique lens; `#8` Credibility tiered; `#9` Corrective conceptual model or methodological agenda proposed; `#10` Boundary conditions acknowledged.

## 7. Stage Quality Checklists (All Modes)
- **Pre-Writing**: Gate 0 locked in `framework_selection.md`; `papers.json` & `literature_matrix.md` complete with provenance; >= 3 citation-backed gaps (0 pseudo-gaps); outline scored >= 28/35; citation style locked.
- **Post-Draft**: Claim-led topic sentence in every paragraph; 1 paragraph = 1 message (4–6 sentences); active voice; mean sentence ~21 words (max 40); `claim_evidence_map.md` complete; Results present synthesis engine output; Final Introduction rewritten after Results; Abstract written last.
- **Stage 5 Style, Grammar & Overlap**: All 26 prose refinement patterns checked (`style_refinement_report.md`); 100% English-US spelling and serial comma (`grammar_report.md`); zero uncited overlap >= 7 consecutive words and 1:1 citation parity (`overlap_and_citation_report.md`); zero unresolved CRITICAL/IMPORTANT items in `peer_review_audit_findings.md`.
