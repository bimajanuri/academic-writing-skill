# Academic Manuscript Output Rubric

Use a 0–4 scale. Scores describe the manuscript, not the effort.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Method/reporting compliance | absent | partial | fully traceable |
| Claim-evidence alignment | major claims unsupported | minor gaps | claims match evidence, design, and modality |
| Citation/source integrity | fabricated or mismatched | some items flagged | citations, bibliography, DOIs, and quotes traceable |
| Factual/data fidelity | data altered or invented | minor uncertainty | methods, numbers, findings, and limits match sources |
| Argument/synthesis quality | disconnected summary | uneven synthesis | clear argument, gap, and contribution |
| English-US correctness | obstructive errors | several errors | clean, consistent academic English-US |
| Humanizer/plagiarism control | strong risk | unresolved patterns | natural, original, cited synthesis |
| Journal/audience fit | unsuitable | broadly suitable | target, structure, terminology, and length fit |

Use 1 and 3 for intermediate cases. Never average away a guardrail failure.
