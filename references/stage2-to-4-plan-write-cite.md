|---|---|---|

## 2. Outline Builder & 7-Dimension Reviewer Rubric (Stages 2.3–2.4)

### 2.1 Word Allocation & Paragraph Planning (`paper_outline.md`)
- **Target Proportions** (for a standard 7,000–8,000 word review manuscript):
  - **Abstract**: 150–250 words (excluded from body%)
  - **1. Introduction**: 15% (~1,000–1,200 words; 5–7 paragraphs)
  - **2. Theoretical Background / Prior Reviews**: 15% (~1,000–1,200 words; 5–7 paragraphs)
  - **3. Methodology**: 15–20% (~1,100–1,500 words; 6–8 paragraphs)
  - **4. Synthesis Results**: 30–35% (~2,200–2,800 words; 10–14 paragraphs + synthesis tables/figures)
  - **5. Discussion & Implications**: 15–20% (~1,100–1,500 words; 6–8 paragraphs)
  - **6. Conclusion**: 5–10% (~400–700 words; 2–4 paragraphs)
- **Topic-Sentence-First Rule**: Write the exact claim-led topic sentence and cited evidence IDs (`[P01, P04, P09]`) for every planned paragraph in `paper_outline.md` before drafting prose.

### 2.2 7-Dimension Reviewer Self-Assessment Rubric (35 Points Total)
Score the outline (Stage 2.4) and final draft (Stage 7.4) from `1–5` per dimension (**require >= 28 / 35** to pass):
1. **Problem Importance & Timeliness** (1–5)
2. **Methodological Fit & Protocol Rigor** (1–5)
3. **Literature Coverage & Search Reproducibility** (1–5)
4. **Synthesis Depth vs. Mere Descriptive Summary** (1–5)
5. **Novelty & Theoretical/Practical Contribution** (1–5)
6. **Logical Flow & Structural Clarity** (1–5)
7. **Target Journal Fit & Readership Value** (1–5)

## 3. Novelty & Contribution Framing (`contribution_statement.md` — Stage 2.7)

Frame contributions using 1–3 of the **Five Valid Novelty Archetypes**:
1. **Taxonomic / Conceptual Synthesis Novelty** (Scoping): First systematic mapping and operational taxonomy of a fragmented domain.
2. **Higher-Order Interpretive Novelty** (Meta-Ethnography): New 3rd-order constructs and line-of-argument theory derived from reciprocal translation.
3. **Debate Reconciliation Novelty** (Narrative): Resolving a longstanding theoretical/empirical contradiction by identifying boundary moderators.
4. **Cross-Paradigm Integration Novelty** (Integrative): Unifying isolated qualitative and quantitative streams into an integrated model.
5. **Methodological / Epistemological Corrective Novelty** (Critical/SLR): Exposing systemic measurement/design biases and establishing a rigorous effect estimate or corrective agenda.

**Anti-Overclaim Rule**: Every contribution statement must follow:
`[Gap in Literature (with >=3 citations)] -> [What This Review Does (method + N studies)] -> [Specific Deliverable (taxonomy / 3rd-order construct / effect synthesis)] -> [Verified Boundary Condition]`.

## 4. Journal Targeting & Legitimacy Verification (Module B — Stage 2.2 & 7.4)

1. **Journal Arena Matching**: Select 3–5 candidate journals in `journal_comparison.md` matching the synthesis mode (e.g., *Systematic Reviews*, *JBI Evidence Synthesis*, *Research Synthesis Methods*, *Qualitative Health Research*, or domain-specific Q1/Q2 journals that publish reviews).
2. **Journal Positioning Blueprint**: Analyze 3–5 recent review articles from the target journal to match typical word count, heading conventions, table count, and citation style.
3. **Journal Legitimacy Verification**: Verify that every candidate journal is actively indexed in Scopus/Web of Science/DOAJ, has a transparent peer-review timeline (not guaranteed 48-hour acceptance), has verifiable editorial board members, and belongs to a recognized publisher or scholarly society.

## 5. Section-by-Section Writing Guide (Stage 3)

### 5.1 Required Drafting Sequence
Always draft in this exact order so the Introduction and Abstract reflect the actual synthesis findings:
`1. Draft 0 Introduction (scaffolding) -> 2. Literature Review / Background -> 3. Methodology -> 4. Results (Synthesis Engine Output) -> 5. Discussion -> 6. Conclusion -> 7. Final Introduction (Rewritten) -> 8. Abstract (Last)`.

### 5.2 Section Architecture Politics
- **Introduction (5-Move Funnel)**:
  - *Move 1*: Phenomenon importance and concrete domain stakes (cite 2–3 recent anchor papers).
  - *Move 2*: What prior research/reviews have established.
  - *Move 3*: The specific evidence-backed gap (`gap_matrix.md`) and why existing reviews fall short.
  - *Move 4*: Objective, research questions (mode framework), and synthesis methodology used.
  - *Move 5*: Verified contributions (2–3 bulleted or numbered takeaways) and manuscript roadmap.
- **Literature Review / Theoretical Background**: Define core constructs, trace prior reviews, and justify the conceptual lens. Never dump a chronological list of study abstracts.
- **Methodology**: Report study design & reporting standard (`PRISMA 2020`, `PRISMA-ScR`, `eMERGe`, `SANRA`, `Whittemore & Knafl`), information sources & exact search dates, full Boolean query string, inclusion/exclusion table, screening & deduplication flow, extraction & provenance procedure, quality appraisal tool, and synthesis engine method.
- **Results**: Present study selection flow, characteristics of included studies (`literature_matrix.md` summary), quality appraisal summary, and the **Synthesis Engine subsections** (Conceptual Taxonomy, 3rd-Order Construct Translation, Camp Comparison, or Subgroup/Meta-Analytic Synthesis).
- **Discussion**: (1) Principal synthesis findings vs. prior literature, (2) Theoretical implications, (3) Practical/policy implications, (4) Strengths & limitations of the included evidence and review process, (5) Concrete future research agenda table.
- **Conclusion**: Concise synthesis answer to the RQs and closing takeaway (no new citations or statistics).

## 6. Citation & Bibliography Formatter (Stage 4)

- Enforce 1:1 bi-directional validation (`citation_validation.md`) between every in-text citation and `bibliography.md`.
- Trace every direct quotation (especially 1st-order constructs in Meta-Ethnography) with author, year, and page/section locator (`(Author, Year, p. X)` or `[n, p. X]`).
- Include active DOIs formatted per the chosen style (`APA 7`, `MLA 9`, `Turabian`, `Vancouver`, `Harvard`, `Chicago`, or `IEEE` — see `templates/citation-style-templates.md`).
